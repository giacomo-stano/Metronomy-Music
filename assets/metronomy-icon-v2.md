## Prompt finale

Create one finished iPhone music-player app icon. The user explicitly prefers the Apple Music aesthetic: familiar, very clean, minimal, polished iOS icon design. A single white musical eighth note centered on a rich rose-red background with a very restrained smooth tonal shift from brighter pink-red at the top to deeper crimson-red at the bottom. The note must be unmistakably musical and beautifully proportioned: one gently slanted upright stem, one substantial rounded oval notehead at the lower left, and one elegantly curved flag at the upper right. Use a custom simple silhouette rather than copying the Apple Music double-note logo. Flat solid white symbol with clean edges and gently softened junctions, no internal decoration. Balanced optical centering, symbol about 55 percent of canvas height, ample negative space. Square full-bleed opaque artwork, background reaches every edge; no baked-in rounded outer corners. No letters, no M monogram, no text, no vinyl, no headphones, no metronome, no surrounding objects. No glass, no glow, no texture, no 3D, no bevel, no drop shadow. Only one standalone app icon, no mockup, no contact sheet, no presentation board.
